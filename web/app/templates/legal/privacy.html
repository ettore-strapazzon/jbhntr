4. How long we keep it

  • Account and profile — until you delete your account.
  • Uploaded documents — until you delete them or your account.
  • Searches and results — 12 months, then deleted automatically.
  • Page views — 24 months, aggregated.

5. How we protect it

Uploaded files are encrypted at rest. Passwords are hashed with bcrypt and are never recoverable. All traffic is over HTTPS. Access is restricted and every query is scoped to your own account.

6. Your rights

You can access, correct, export, restrict, object to, or erase your data, and withdraw consent at any time.

  • Access / portability — download everything we hold
  • Erasure — delete your account, which is a real deletion
  • Correction — edit anything on your profile

You may also complain to your data-protection authority (in Italy, the Garante).

7. Cookies

One strictly-necessary cookie keeps you signed in. Analytics is cookieless. See Cookies.

8. Automated decision-making

We score and rank jobs automatically. This produces recommendations only — it has no legal or similarly significant effect on you, makes no hiring decision, and is never shared with employers. You can ignore any result, and your feedback adjusts future rankings.

9. Changes

We'll tell you about material changes by email or in-app before they take effect.
