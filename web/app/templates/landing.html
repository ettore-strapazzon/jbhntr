{# ============================ PROBLEM (HOME-004) ============================ #}

THE PROBLEM

The right role is rarely in one place.

It may be on a major job board, a niche site or a company career page you did not know to check. Alerts send every keyword hit. JBHNTR reads across the market, removes duplicates and weak fits, and gives you the small set worth a decision.

  • Stop rebuilding the same search across different platforms.
  • Stop opening roles that fail your location, level or working constraints.
  • See adjacent roles that title-only alerts miss.

{# ======================= DIFFERENTIATORS (HOME-006) ======================= #}

WHY IT IS DIFFERENT

Judgement in two directions, not a longer feed.

  • Judges fit both ways. A role can suit your goals and still be a stretch on the requirements. Both scores are shown, with the reasoning, instead of one number that hides the trade-off.
  • Understands the work, not only the title. Semantic retrieval finds adjacent and equivalent roles, while hard constraints keep the list grounded in where and how you can actually work.
  • Searches outside one platform. Broad job sources, niche boards and employer ATS pages, including Greenhouse, Lever and Ashby boards.
  • Learns what you reject. Ratings, notes, saves, dismissals and applications become context for later scans.
