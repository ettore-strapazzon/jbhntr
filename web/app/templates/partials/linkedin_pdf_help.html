{# How to pull a CV PDF from LinkedIn. Many users don't know the "Save to PDF" path and it's buried, so spell it out with a link to their own profile. #}
No CV file? Get one from your LinkedIn profile
  1. Open your LinkedIn profile (or LinkedIn → Me → View profile).
  2. Click More under your name and headline, then Save to PDF.
  3. Upload that PDF here — LinkedIn builds a resume-style PDF from your profile.
