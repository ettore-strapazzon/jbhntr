How it works

  1. Share your link. Send it by message, email or chat. It carries your code, so we know the invite came from you.
  2. They sign up and run their first scan.{% if first_scan_free %} Their first scan is free, and your link adds {{ friend_credits }} bonus credits to their account.{% endif %}
  3. You get {{ inviter_credits }} credits. We add them two to three days after your friend's first scan, once they've also saved, dismissed, rated or marked a role as applied, and our usual checks are clear.

There's no fixed limit on how many friends you invite or how many credits you earn. Your credits are added two to three days after a friend's first scan, once they've used their results and our checks are clear. If a lot of your invites come through in one month, we review the extra ones by hand before adding their credits, so those can take longer. Self-invites don't count, and credits from duplicate or throwaway accounts may be held or removed. Credits stay on your account: they have no cash value and can't be sold, transferred or exchanged for cash. If you share publicly, say that you earn credits from your link (the messages below already do). Terms
