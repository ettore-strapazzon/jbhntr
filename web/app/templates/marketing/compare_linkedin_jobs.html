JBHNTR and LinkedIn Jobs, compared

LinkedIn Jobs is a professional network with its own job inventory and, now, AI-assisted search. JBHNTR is an independent agent that searches across many sources and scores each role in both directions. They solve different halves of the same problem, and most people will use both.

When LinkedIn is the better tool

Sometimes it is the right choice: when the role is likely to be posted only on LinkedIn, when the network connection matters more than the match, and when a recruiter needs to find the candidate rather than the other way round. JBHNTR does not have broader coverage than LinkedIn. The difference is structural: LinkedIn earns from the employer and recruiter side, so it is not built to be an agent acting for one candidate across the whole market.
