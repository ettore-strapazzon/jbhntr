# About me (rename this file to `about_me.md` and fill it in)

**What this file is for:** free-text background that only the AI reads. It is
used for two things — judging *nuance* the structured filters can't capture, and
**writing your tailored CV and cover letters in your own voice**.

**What NOT to put here:** locations, salary floor, seniority, keywords, or job
type. Those live in `config/profile.yaml` (the tool filters on them in code).
Don't repeat them here.

Write naturally — a few short paragraphs is plenty. Nothing is mandatory.

---

## My story so far
What you've actually done, in your own words: the arc of your career, the kinds
of problems you've solved, what you were hired for vs. what you ended up owning.

## What I'm proud of
Two or three things you'd genuinely want a hiring manager to know — a project
you led, an outcome you moved, something you built from nothing.

## How I like to work
Team size and culture that suits you, how much autonomy you want, IC vs.
managing, pace, remote/office working style.

## What I want more of / less of
Technologies, domains, or responsibilities you want to grow into — and the ones
you're actively trying to move away from.

## Anything a CV can't show
Career gaps and their reason, a career change and the "why", languages you
speak, visa/work-authorization situation, or anything else worth context.
