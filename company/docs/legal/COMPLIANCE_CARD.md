# Compliance Card — JBHNTR

Owner: GC · v1.1 (2026-11-13: + C11, match-card review, market-read/invite rules) · **The company lints every outbound surface against this card.** Each rule is checkable: a human (or a lint pass) can look at an artefact and answer yes/no. A "no" blocks the surface until fixed. GC clears the exceptions.

> Not legal advice. These are the company's self-imposed honesty rules, tightened to the GTM posture (CHARTER Guardrail 5, GTM §2, §5.1, §7). A one-hour session with an Italian lawyer before public launch is still worth it.

## The rules (lint checklist)

| # | Rule | Check (pass = yes) | Applies to |
|---|---|---|---|
| C1 | **Disclose we build the tool.** Any public mention of the product/space discloses that we build a tool in it — the "who are you?" paragraph (GTM §2) or an inline "(disclosure: I work on JBHNTR)". | Does every post/comment/reply/bio that names the product or the job-search space carry a disclosure? | Reddit, HN, X, LinkedIn, comments, DMs, bios, FAQ |
| C2 | **No fake accounts or personas.** Every account is a real, disclosed brand or person account. No sockpuppets, no invented team members, no fake reviewers, no fake "I'm just a user who found this" posts. | Is every account traceable to the brand or to Ettore, and never posing as an unaffiliated third party? | all channels, reviews, directories |
| C3 | **Respect each community's promo rules.** Before posting to a community, read its self-promotion rule; post only within it. Where a community bans links/promo, participate without the link (answer first, link only if the rule allows). Where automation is banned, post manually. | Is there a per-community rule-check line on the draft, and does the draft obey it? | Reddit, HN, forums, Slack/Discord communities |
| C4 | **Honest comparison tables.** Every row is a real, current, verifiable fact about the named competitor. No cherry-picked framing that misstates what they do; if they do a thing, the table says so. We can be favourable; we cannot be false. | Can each cell be sourced to the competitor's live product/site as of the stated date? Is the "last checked" date on the page? | /compare/* pages, ads, decks |
| C5 | **"Source-available", never "open-source (OSI)".** The public repo is described as "source-available" under BSL 1.1 / Elastic 2.0. Never call it "open source" unqualified, never imply an OSI-approved licence. Existing MIT-licensed public code stays MIT to whoever already has it; the new licence applies from the split forward. | Does every description of the licence say "source-available", not "open source"? | README, LICENSE, site, posts, directories |
| C6 | **Never imply auto-apply.** We score and shortlist with reasons for/against; the user applies. No copy, screenshot, or feature name may state or imply we apply to jobs for the user. "Two-way fit", "shortlist", "worth pursuing" — never "we apply", "auto-apply", "applies for you". | Does the surface avoid any claim or implication that the product submits applications? | product UI, landing, ads, /compare/auto-apply-tools, posts |
| C7 | **Numbers must be real and sourced.** Every statistic (activation %, "71% say better", user counts, "1,840 roles, 14 worth it") is a measured number with a source/date behind it. `—` means not measured; never a guess, never a rounded-up projection stated as fact. Cumulative vs current is stated honestly (e.g. "currently active", not total signups). | Can each number be traced to KPIS.md / a query / a dated study? Is any forecast labelled as a forecast, not a fact? | all external claims, press, partner copy, ads, data drops |
| C8 | **No undisclosed incentive.** If a share/review/referral earns the sharer credits or money, that incentive is disclosed where required (reviews, testimonials, endorsements). Referral mechanics are disclosed on the /invite surface. | Is any incentivised endorsement/review/testimonial disclosed as incentivised? | referrals, reviews, testimonials, influencer/partner posts |
| C9 | **Brand-led, not founder-led.** Ettore is credited only as a builder (README/LICENSE/commits/humans.txt, "Built by Ettore Strapazzon"), never titled "Founder/CEO"; no PR around the person; no fronting Product Hunt. Exception: Ettore's own LinkedIn for 1:1 PMF chats as "a personal project I'm testing". | Does the surface avoid giving Ettore a company title or building PR around the person? | all public surfaces, PH, press |
| C10 | **Privacy promises are kept.** We don't claim a privacy/security posture we haven't implemented. "Anonymised" share actually strips the identifying fields; "we don't sell your data" is true; named controller + privacy@ live before any cohort beyond friends. | Does every privacy/security claim match what the product actually does today? | product, privacy/terms, security copy, ads |
| C11 | **Public share artefacts are private-by-default and revocable** (added 2026-11-13). Anything reachable without login (`/m/`, `/r/`, their PNGs) shows only what the sharer saw in a preview and chose; identifying/optional fields default **off**; the sharer can **stop sharing** (link + image 404 at once); account deletion removes the page **and** the image; page `noindex` + image `X-Robots-Tag: noindex`; images are never cached under the public `/static` mount or committed to git. | Can the sharer see exactly what is public, switch it off, and is nothing left behind? | match card, market read, any future public artefact |

## How to use it
- **CMO/CPO:** self-lint every draft against C1–C10 before filing it for the Founder. Put a one-line `rule-check:` on each digest draft (R1-09 already requires this).
- **GC:** clears edge cases and any surface that can't cleanly answer "yes". Blocks (doesn't soften) a "no"; states what would unblock it.
- **Review cadence:** GC re-reads this card every Monday against new surfaces; updates in place when a rule proves too weak or a community's rule changes.

### B. Rules the market read (`/r/{token}`) must follow, before it ships
- **MR1. Real counts only (C7).** Show that `Search`'s actual `raw_count → located_count → ranked_count → scored_count`. Labels describe what was measured: "roles scanned", never "every live role" or "the whole market". A running or failed scan shows "not ready", never zeros or estimates. No generic numbers ("~15 worth your time"); only this read's numbers.
- **MR2. No market-wide claims from one read.** Don't write "only 0.8% of jobs are worth applying to" or similar generalisations unless backed by a dated corpus query (C7).
- **MR3. v2 reason histogram** only from persisted real counts (`reject_reason`), with unclassified reasons shown as "other". No invented buckets (ARTEFACTS_SPEC §2.3).
- **MR4. Anonymise toggle, default ON, and it must hold (C10/C11).** ON = no name, no employer names (current or prospective), target function only (e.g. "Product"), locations at country/region level. OFF may add the derived role title and city. **Never, either way:** CV text, email, salary, visa/right-to-work, years of experience, free-text objective, or the list of companies. The same toggle governs the OG PNG, which re-renders on change.
- **MR5. Preview + stop sharing + deletion cleanup**, identical to card changes #2–#6: preview of exactly what is public, `unshare` → 404 for page and image, account deletion removes the PNG, cache outside `/static`, `X-Robots-Tag: noindex` on the image, `noindex` on the page, git-ignored.
- **MR6. CTA + incentive.** "Get your own market read — first scan free" is cleared while `FIRST_SCAN_FREE=true`. It carries the same referral disclosure line as card change #8. Any pre-written caption follows the card caption pattern (#1): no absolutes, incentive disclosed.

**In-flight R3-02 check (uncommitted `routes/market_read.py`, `r_read.html`, `read_share_sheet.html`, read 2026-11-13).** Already compliant: counts are real, not-ready state, anonymise default ON with countries only, no employer/CV text (MR1/MR3/MR4). Fix before merge:
- `og_description` *"Every live role scanned and scored both ways. …"* → `One scan of the live market, narrowed both ways. Get your own market read — first scan free.` (MR1)
- `r_read.html` funnel note *"Every role is scored both ways — …"* is false, because only the shortlisted roles are scored → `The shortlisted roles are scored both ways: how they fit what the job seeker wants and how the job seeker fits what the employer asks.` (MR1)
- `_caption` → `I ran a JBHNTR scan: {raw:,} live roles, narrowed to {scored:,} worth a decision. My market read: {url} (Sign up from the read and you get bonus credits; I get some once you run your first scan.)` (MR6)
- `r_read.html` CTA sub-line *"… No card."* → `See how many live roles are actually worth your time. First scan free, no credit card.` plus the card #8 referral line when a code is present. (MR6)
- Headline/OG wording: "worth your time" / "worth it" vs the stage label "Worth a decision". Use `worth a decision` everywhere for consistency (LOW).
- MR5 not met yet: no unshare, PNG cached under `/static/og/` (`r-{token}.png`), no `X-Robots-Tag`, not removed on account deletion. Apply the card #4/#6 fixes once, in the shared helpers (`mint_share_token` / `serve_og_png`), so both artefacts inherit them.

### C. Rules the `/invite` page must follow, before it ships
- **IN1. Reward wording mirrors the code, numbers from config (C7/C8).** Use exactly: `Everyone's first scan is free. When a friend signs up with your link, they get {REFERRAL_FRIEND_CREDITS} bonus credits straight away, and you get {REFERRAL_CREDITS} credits once they complete their first scan.` Do **not** present "first scan free" as a referral perk; ARTEFACTS_SPEC §3.1's "Your friend gets their first scan free + 5 credits" must change to the line above.
- **IN2. Conditions on the page (C8, TERMS §6).** `Credits have no cash value and can't be sold or transferred. We may hold or remove credits from self-referrals, duplicate or fake accounts.` State a payout delay or monthly cap **only if the code enforces it**. Today `reward_inviter_if_activated` pays immediately with no ceiling, while TERMS_DRAFT §6 promises "delayed payout … soft monthly ceiling". The code and the terms must match before either `/invite` or `/terms` goes public. CTO and GC decide which side moves.
- **IN3. Pre-written messages: no absolutes, incentive disclosed in every one.** Banned: "every live role", "~15 worth your time", any user or success stat not in KPIS. Cleared templates:
  - LinkedIn/X/Bluesky: `I've been testing JBHNTR. It scores each job both ways, with reasons for and against, so you see the few worth your time. First scan's free, and signing up with my link gets us both bonus credits: {invite_url}`
  - WhatsApp: `Trying JBHNTR for job hunting, first scan's free and my link gets us both bonus credits: {invite_url}`
  - Email (`mailto:`, sent from the user's own client): subject `A job-search tool I'm trying`, body `It scores each role both ways, how it fits what you want and how you fit what they ask, with reasons for and against. First scan's free. If you sign up with my link you get bonus credits, and I get some once you run your first scan: {invite_url}`
  - **Reddit: no referral template at all.** Most subreddits ban referral links (C3). Offer none, or a plain `https://jbhntr.app` with the C1 disclosure.
- **IN4. Live counter shows counts only.** "N signed up · M ran their first scan · +X credits". Never the names or emails of invited people, and no leaderboard of other users. Use the label "ran their first scan", not "activated" (the internal KPI definition differs).
- **IN5. No incentive tied to public endorsement.** No credits for reviews, ratings, posts or tagging. No cash or cash equivalents. No "enter your friend's email and we'll invite them": if we send it, we are the sender of unsolicited marketing (PECR reg 22 / ePrivacy Art 13 [verify]). Needs separate GC review.
- **IN6.** `/invite` is authenticated and `noindex`.
