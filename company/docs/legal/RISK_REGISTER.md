## Escalation summary
- **RED (R-007) — accepted by Founder until revenue/Phase-2 (R-005):** incorporation gap. Score stays 16 RED (likelihood × impact unchanged); the *treatment* is now **Accept + monitor** by explicit Founder decision — incorporation lands at revenue / before Phase-2 paid credits, and the Founder accepts the residual controller + footer exposure (on Ettore personally) through the public spikes in the meantime. GC keeps `/privacy`, `/terms`, data drop and Show HN gated on a named controller being live, and re-raises if a spike is imminent or the exposure materialises. Only the Founder (with a lawyer/accountant) executes the incorporation.
- **ORANGE (R-001, R-003, R-004, R-005, R-008):** active mitigation owned as above; GC reviews weekly; escalate to Founder if a trigger fires (e.g. a platform cease-and-desist, a DSAR we can't answer, an abuse spike).
- **YELLOW (R-002, R-006, R-009):** mitigate and monitor; promote to ORANGE on trigger.
