## Our wedge (one line)
Everyone else optimises **application volume or formatting**. JBHNTR optimises **selection** — the two-way fit score with reasons, across sources including company career pages, with no auto-apply. "1,840 roles, 14 worth it" is a sentence only we can say, and impossible for an auto-apply tool to produce.
