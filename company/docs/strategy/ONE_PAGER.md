# JBHNTR — one-pager

Owner: CEO · v1 (2026-10-25)

## What
JBHNTR is an AI job-search agent for experienced professionals. It scans every live role across many sources, scores each **two ways** — "fits what you want" and "you fit what they need" — and returns the 10–20 worth a decision, each with the reasons **for and against**. It then tailors a CV and cover letter per role and tracks applications. It **never auto-applies**. Live at **jbhntr.app**.

## The problem
Mid-senior professionals drown in listings. The honest numbers: ~1,840 "matching" roles collapse to ~14 worth a decision. Alerts send every keyword hit; auto-apply tools spray applications that convert worse past ~30. People waste hours and apply to the wrong roles.

## Who
Mid-senior professionals (~5–15 yrs) in generalist functions — product, engineering, data, marketing/growth, sales, ops/strategy, finance, design, people. Markets: UK/IE → EU → US → Canada. Not junior, not deep-specialist verticals.

## Why it's different
1. **Two-way fit with reasons** — a decision, not a relevance list ("1,840 roles, 14 worth it").
2. **Direct career-page coverage** — roles that title-only alerts miss.
3. **No auto-apply** — a control tool, the opposite of spray-and-pray; also the ethical/positioning line.
4. **First scan free, credits after** — no card to try; frictionless.
5. **GDPR-first, European** — a selling point against US-centric competitors.
6. **Brand-led, open-core** — a source-available public shell credits the builder; the matching engine (sources, prompts, anti-abuse, corpus) stays private.

## Traction plan (Phase 1 — 13 weeks, ≤ USD 1,000)
Brand-led GTM, no founder marketing: shareable artefacts (match card, "my market read", invite) → disclosed community participation + directories + comparison pages fed by a daily draft digest → PMF interviews + referral loop → partnerships (coaches, layoff cohorts, tools) → data-drop + Show HN spikes. **Goal: 1,000 activated users** (profile ≥70% + first search). Gates: ≥60% say results beat how they search today; K-factor ≥ 0.3.

## Business model
Credits. First scan free; scans and tailored CV/CL cost credits. **Phase 2** turns on paid credit top-ups once the gate holds and the loop works. CFO maintains the unit economics (compute cost/active user vs credit revenue) and the path to break-even → profitability.
