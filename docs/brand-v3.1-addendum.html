v3.1 · 01 · Changelog

What v3.1 changes, and why.

Six moves. Each raises restraint and precision — never saturation. The instant JBHNTR looks loud, the honest-broker position cracks. Premium here means considered, not decorated.

01
Layered depth. Elevation is now three stacked, warm navy-tinted shadows so light falls off gradually. The single biggest "expensive" cue, at zero cost.
02
A lit ground. The warm-white canvas carries two very faint tints — cornflower and apricot — so the page reads as a surface, not a flat fill.
03
Dimensional controls. Primary buttons gain a 1px inner top-highlight and a hover lift; flat controls stay flat. Tactile, not plastic.
04
The verdict. A new first-class brand object: the two-way judgement — Apply now / Worth a stretch / Watch out — now leads the match card.
05
Match strength. One 1–4 pip glyph + one word (Excellent / Strong / Fair / Long shot), unified across card, headers, filters and emails.
06
Apricot earns its job. The warm accent maps to one idea only — judgement — and now owns the strongest verdict.
v3.1 · 02 · Surfaces

Depth, not saturation.

The uplift is material, not chromatic. Warm-tinted layered shadows, hairlines in the paper's own hue, and a barely-there lit ground. You feel the depth before you notice it.

Elevation — raised
--elev-1: 0 1px 1px rgba(23,51,75,.05), 0 2px 4px rgba(23,51,75,.05), 0 8px 16px rgba(23,51,75,.05); --elev-2: 0 1px 2px rgba(23,51,75,.06), 0 6px 12px rgba(23,51,75,.08), 0 20px 40px -8px rgba(23,51,75,.14);

Three layers, not one. The tint is navy, never grey — grey shadow on warm paper reads muddy.

The lit ground
background: radial-gradient(1100px 620px at 12% -10%, color-mix(in srgb,#7599DB 7%,transparent), transparent 60%), radial-gradient(950px 520px at 100% 0%, color-mix(in srgb,#E8A67E 6%,transparent), transparent 55%), #FBF8F2; background-attachment: fixed;

Under 8% each. Full-bleed bands paint over it; content columns let it whisper through.

Do

  • Warm-tinted, multi-layer shadow for depth.
  • Hairlines in the paper's hue (#E4DED2), not grey.
  • Keep tints faint enough to doubt they're there.

Never

  • Glassmorphism, blur panels, or glow.
  • Animated or high-contrast gradient backgrounds.
  • A fourth colour. Premium is precision with the three we have.
v3.1 · 03 · Controls

Dimensional, not plastic.

A 1px top highlight makes the solid fill read as a lit surface; a soft elevation on hover lifts it a hair. Reserved for primary actions — flat controls (ghost, link, chips, toggles) opt out so the hierarchy stays legible.

v3.1 · 04 · The verdict — new brand object

Judgement, in two directions.

The match card already scores fit in two directions. v3.1 promotes their synthesis to the headline: a single verdict that tells the reader what to do. It is the landing page's promise, kept on the product. This is a brand object now — the same four words appear on the card, in decks, in the press kit and in email.

Apply now
You fit, and the role fits you.
Worth a stretch
The role fits you; you don't fit yet.
Watch out
You fit; the role doesn't fit you.
Not shown
Neither direction fits — it never surfaces.
Derivation (T = 65)
fit_role = "fits what you want" fit_cand = "you fit their requirements" both ≥ T → Apply now role ≥ T, cand < T → Worth a stretch cand ≥ T, role < T → Watch out
Colour = meaning

Stretch · corn-700
Watch · attention

Apricot is reserved for Apply now — the strongest judgement. It never decorates.

v3.1 · 05 · Match strength

How strong, at a glance.

The verdict says what kind of match. Strength says how strong — the priority read from the score. It is one 1–4 pip glyph and one word, identical on the card, the group headers, the filters, emails and here. Pips, not stars: stars read as a consumer review or a hire probability, which the brand disclaims.

Excellent
85–100 · navy 800
Strong
70–84 · corn 800
Fair
55–69 · corn 500
Long shot
< 55 · navy 300
Derivation
pips = [4,3,2,1] by tier (tier 1 = 4 pips) word = tier_word(tier) // at render time, so a // relabel needs no re-scan

Pips carry the tier colour; the word stays a readable ink (a coloured “Fair” would fail contrast).

One lexicon, everywhere

Card · group headers · filters · emails · this book. It replaces the old mix (Excellent / Strong / Possible / Weak, and Apply now / Strong / Possible), which also collided with the verdict’s “Apply now”.

Strength and verdict answer different questions and sit together on the card: pips + word beside the score, the verdict as the headline.

v3.1 · 07 · The accent

Apricot earns its one job.

In v3.0 apricot marked "judgement" in a handful of places. v3.1 gives it a single, defensible owner: the moment of decision — the Apply now verdict and the primary act of applying. An accent that maps 1:1 to a brand idea reads as intent; scattered, it reads as decoration. This is a reduction, and reductions are what make a system feel expensive.

Owns

The Apply now verdict · the apply CTA · the "judgement" rule under the wordmark.

Never

Decorative fills · non-decision buttons · body text · more than one moment per view.

Value

apr-700 #C4743F
on dark: apr-400 #F0BA98
